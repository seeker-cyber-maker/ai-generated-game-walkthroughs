The Rebel Flagship: 3-Phase Destruction Guide [BOSS]

Phase 1: Artillery Isolation

Target the Triple Missile Artillery (3rd pod from left) first. CRITICAL EXPLOIT: Leave the operator of the Triple Laser pod (2nd room) alive to prevent the automated Flagship AI from activating!

Phase 2: Drone Surge Management

Bypass the Defense Drone by depowering Hacking as the drone fires, then repower. Activate Level 1 Cloaking when the Drone Power Surge alarm sounds.

Phase 3: Zoltan Shield & 12-Laser Surge

Strip the 12 HP Zoltan Shield with Flak/Ion. Counter Mind Control with your own Mind Control or Slug crew. Cloak the 12-laser Power Surge.
