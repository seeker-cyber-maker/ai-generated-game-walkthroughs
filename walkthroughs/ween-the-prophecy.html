2. Game Basics, Companion Roles & The 30-Day Clock [BASE]

In the mystical Blue Land, the wicked sorcerer Kraal will unleash eternal darkness on the 30th day. Only Ween, guided by Petroy, can gather the 3 Grains of Sand and activate the Great Hourglass of Revuss in time!

CompanionSpecialized Tactical Role
WeenApprentice wizard. Combines reagents in the mortar, casts spells (Morphosys, Luciferys, Vitalys), and mixes items in the haversack.
PetroyMentor creature. Emits acoustic echoes to scare bats and manipulates delicate magical devices.
OrainMighty warrior. Lifts heavy stone blocks and operates mechanical forge levers.
UrmNimble rogue. Climbs trees, slips into narrow crevices, and retrieves distant objects.

5. The Critical-Path Minimalist Route [FAST]

STEP 01 [Porch] ────────────► Forage Straw, Wooden Sticks, Strawberries.
STEP 02 [Porch Mechanism] ──► Stick on gutter -> Unlatch door -> Enter.
STEP 03 [Laboratory] ───────► Take Brass Key, Mortar -> Cook Strawberry Jam.
STEP 04 [Cellar] ───────────► Unlock door -> Take Flask, Mandrake, Sulfur.
STEP 05 [Forest Crossroads] ► Cut Shinbone Spear -> Get Woodpecker Feather.
STEP 06 [Pine Tree] ────────► Gather sticky Amber Resin.
STEP 07 [Inventory Mortar] ─► Grind Mandrake + Sulfur -> Awakening Dust.
STEP 08 [Golem Orgol] ──────► Blow Dust in Golem eyes -> GET GRAIN 1 (GOLD).
STEP 09 [Ant Mound] ────────► Mongoose + Jam to Queen -> Pass tunnel.
STEP 10 [Bat Cavern] ───────► Petroy squeaks -> Scrape Guano -> Set Prism.
STEP 11 [Smelting Forge] ───► Orain pumps bellows -> Melt gold -> Cast Key.
STEP 12 [Dragon Altar] ─────► Lure dragon with Resin -> Unlock -> GRAIN 2.
STEP 13 [Worm's Hollow] ────► Brew Chamomile Tea in Cauldron -> Cure Worm.
STEP 14 [Citadel Moat] ─────► Glue + Firefly on Twig -> Urm drops drawbridge.
STEP 15 [Kraal Lab] ────────► Reflect dark bolt with Mirror -> GET GRAIN 3.
STEP 16 [Hourglass Tower] ──► Solve pendulum rope -> Insert 3 Grains.
STEP 17 [Revuss Finale] ────► Rotate Hourglass of Revuss -> 100% VICTORY!

7. Engine Forensics: Coktel's Gob Engine Decompilation [ENGN]

COKTEL VISION GOB ENGINE ARCHITECTURE:
  • INTRO.STK (7.12 MB): Monolithic resource container containing all screen
    backgrounds, sprite animations, and audio effects.
  • ALL.ASK (11 KB): Bytecode script table evaluating triggers, companion
    actions, and state transitions.
  • OBJET1.CAT - OBJET4.CAT: Dynamic item catalogs across the 4 acts.
  • *.GDR Display Drivers: LVGA.GDR (256-color VGA), LEGA.GDR (16-color EGA).

ScummVM Compatibility Profile:
  • Target Engine ID: gob (Sub-engine target: ween).
  • Normalizes CPU-dependent 30-day timer cycles.

8. Cultural Retrospective: Why Ween Remained Obscure [HIST]

The French "Puzzle Chamber" Paradigm: Unlike American point-and-click games that prioritized large interconnected worlds, French developers like Coktel Vision built self-contained, mathematically dense puzzle boxes that required precise sequencing.
