6. The Manuals, Lore Books, Feelies & Copy Protection [MANL]

The Player's Guide: 48-page manual explaining Gladstone politics, character creation, weapon mechanics, and the 4 spell disciplines.

The History of the Lands (Narrative Feelie): An illustrated lorebook detailing the history of King Richard Leene, the Draracle, and Scotia discovering the Nether Mask.

Off-Disk Copy Protection (Floppy vs CD):

  • 1993 Floppy Edition: Required answering manual look-up questions ("Turn to Page X, Line Y, enter the Z-th word") before leaving Gladstone Keep.
  • 1994 CD-ROM Talkie Edition (v1.23): Westwood completely removed the copy protection prompt, as the physical CD-ROM served as hardware DRM.

7. Engine Forensics & Binary Reverse-Engineering [ENGN]

Bit 6 (0x0040): Illusion Wall (Passable stone)
