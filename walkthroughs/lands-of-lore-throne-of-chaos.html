2. Complete Step-by-Step Walkthrough [WLK00]

Act I: Gladstone Keep & The Great Forests [WLK01]

Area Item & Chest Checklist: Act I
  1. Gladstone Briefing: Speak to King Richard in the throne room for the commission and Signet Ring. Recruit Timothy in the east corridor.
  2. Secret Cellar Stash: In the cellar hall, click the third wall torch at (X:08, Y:02) to open the alcove (take Fine Broadsword & Leather Cuirass).
  3. Northlands Forest Secret: Travel to (X:14, Y:08). Click the hollow knot on the third ancient oak tree exactly 3 times to open the clearing containing the Elven Longbow and Lightning Arrows.
    4. POINT OF NO RETURN: You MUST collect the Elven Longbow before completing Roland's Manor. Once Scotia attacks, the forest is burned and the bow is permanently missable!
  4. Roland's Manor Ambush: Drink from the Blue Fountain at (X:04, Y:12) (+5 Permanent Max Mana). Scotia strikes, stealing the Nether Mask and poisoning King Richard.

Act II: Gorkha Swamp & Shaman's Trial [WLK02]

Area Item & Chest Checklist: Act II
  1. Recruit Baccata: Meet Baccata at the Southlands outpost. Equip him with dual weapons (4-arm advantage).
  2. Grey Eagle Inn: Recruit Lora the Healer. Purchase the Compass from the merchant.
  3. Gorkha Swamp Core: Drink from the Green Fountain at (X:08, Y:19) (+5 Permanent Max HP).
  4. Shaman's Gate & Bloodmoss: Deliver a Ruby to the Shaman at (X:18, Y:04) for the healing herb and gate access. Harvest Bloodmoss at (X:22, Y:19).

Act III: Mines of Apparitions & The Draracle [WLK03]

Area Item & Chest Checklist: Act III
  1. Urbish Dwarven Vault: In Mines L1, use the Oil Flask on the rusty track lever at (X:12, Y:04) to open the Dwarven Vault (Warhammer of Crushing & Mithril Mail).
  2. Retrieve Crucible: On Mines L3, defeat the Apparitions and take the Crucible of Faith at (X:16, Y:28).
  3. Draracle Trials: Complete the Scale Tribute, answer the Sphinx riddle, and walk through the illusion wall at (X:24, Y:10). Lora is abducted by Scotia.

Act IV: The White Tower & Gladstone Siege [WLK04]

Area Item & Chest Checklist: Act IV
  1. White Tower Ascent: Drink from the Golden Chalice at (X:16, Y:04) (+2 AC & debuff immunity). Fill Flask with Sweet Water at (X:08, Y:14).
  2. Companion Selection: Choose between Paul (Heavy Fighter) or Dawn (Battle-Mage).
  3. Synthesize King's Elixir: Combine Crucible of Faith, Sweet Water, Bloodmoss, and Silver Goblet in inventory to brew the Elixir of Arin. Cure King Richard to receive the Ruby of Truth.

Act V & VI: Yvel & Castle Cimmeria Finale [WLK05/WLK06]

Area Item & Chest Checklist: Acts V & VI
  1. Yvel Sewers Secret: Click the cracked brick at (X:11, Y:24) for the Ring of Invisibility. Receive Vaelan's Cube from the Elders.
  2. Cimmeria Infiltration: Dispel barriers with Vaelan's Cube. Equip Baccata with the Warhammer of Crushing vs Iron Golems.
  3. The Scotia Boss Battle:
    • Phase 1: Click Ruby of Truth to strip her Nether Mask and beast forms.
    • Phase 2: Activate Vaelan's Cube to reflect her Death Magic back onto her while attacking to claim victory.

For speedrunners and fast replays. Zero item detours, zero side-quest grinding. Strictly the required state-machine geodesic:

STEP 05 [Gorkha Mire] ───────► Walk to (X:22, Y:19), harvest Bloodmoss.
STEP 06 [Urbish Mines L3] ───► Run to (X:16, Y:28), pick up Crucible of Faith.
STEP 07 [Draracle Lair] ─────► Drop 15 gold coins on scale at (X:09, Y:14).
STEP 08 [Draracle Lair] ─────► Choose "Truth" at riddle gate (X:16, Y:10).
STEP 09 [Draracle Lair] ─────► Walk through illusion wall at (X:24, Y:10).
STEP 10 [White Tower L2] ────► Use empty Flask at cistern (X:08, Y:14) (Sweet Water).
STEP 11 [White Tower L3] ────► Recruit Paul or Dawn at (X:16, Y:04).
STEP 12 [Gladstone Treasury] ► Grab Silver Goblet at (X:02, Y:03).
STEP 13 [Inventory Screen] ──► Combine Crucible + Sweet Water + Bloodmoss + Goblet.

STEP 15 [Yvel Elders] ───────► Enter chamber at (X:15, Y:02) -> Get Vaelan's Cube & Key.
STEP 16 [Castle Cimmeria L1] ► Dispel magic barrier at (X:16, Y:08) with Vaelan's Cube.

STEP 18 [Boss Arena] ────────► Use Ruby of Truth -> Use Vaelan's Cube -> Attack crone -> WIN.

5. Engine Forensics & Binary Reverse-Engineering [ENGN]

How the 2.5D compiled engine encodes dungeon data in LEVELxx.INF:

16-Bit Tile Bitmask:
[ Bit 6: 0x0040 ] -> Illusion Wall (Passable = TRUE)
[ Bit 7: 0x0080 ] -> Switch / Trigger Button (Trips TRIG.TBL event)
