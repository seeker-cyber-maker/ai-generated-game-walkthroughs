[03.00] GAME BASICS, CONTROLS & THE MOOD METER ENGINE                   [BASE]
===============================================================================

+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| THE MOOD METER PERSONALITY REGISTER: v_mood                                 |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| [GREEN DEVIL]   v_mood = 0 : LYING / SARCASTIC                              |
| [YELLOW FACE]   v_mood = 1 : NORMAL / NEUTRAL                               |
| [BLUE ANGEL]    v_mood = 2 : TRUTHFUL / POLITE                              |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------+

A. THE 3-STATE MOOD REGISTER (v_mood):
  1. Lying / Sarcastic (v_mood = 0, Green Devil):
     Malcolm delivers snarky lies and deceit. Required for tricking town
     guards, bluffing Darm in his cellar, and outsmarting pirates.
  2. Normal / Neutral (v_mood = 1, Yellow Face):
     Standard pragmatic interactions. Used for inventory manipulation.
  3. Truthful / Polite (v_mood = 2, Blue Angel):
     Malcolm behaves politely and speaks absolute truth. Mandatory during
     the final Royal Court Trial to prove his innocence to King Brandon.

B. CONTROLS & SHORTCUTS:
  • Left Click : Walk, Pick up items, Interact with NPCs.
  • Right Click: Examine items in inventory or on screen.
  • Tab        : Toggle Mood Meter between Lying, Normal, and Truthful.
  • Spacebar   : Skip dialogue line.
  • ESC / F1   : Save / Load / Options menu.

ROUTE A: THE INFLATABLE PEGASUS FLIGHT (AIR ROUTE):
  1. Exit cottage ruins; enter Toy Maker's shop.
  2. Set Mood to Lying (v_mood = 0). Talk to Toy Maker to distract him. Grab
     the Toy Mouse and Squirrel Wheel.
  3. Visit Blacksmith; take Bellows from the wall.
  4. Take Leather Glove from clothesline; squeeze Sweet Berries for juice.
  5. Locate deflated Pegasus float in forest clearing. Use Bellows 4 times.
  6. Combine Berry Juice + Glove for levitation polish. Apply polish to
     Pegasus; Malcolm climbs aboard and flies across the ocean to Isle of Cats!

ROUTE B: THE PIRATE CAPTAIN'S CHARTER (SEA ROUTE):
  1. Retrieve Bank Draft Slip from Town Hall wastebasket.
  2. Enter Royal Bank with Mood set to Lying. Bluff the teller for 50
     Gold Sovereigns.
  3. Speak to Captain Jean-Claude at the docks; hand over 50 Sovereigns.
  4. Combine parchment with squid ink to create Sea Chart; deliver to Captain.
     The pirate schooner sets sail immediately for the Isle of Cats.

ROUTE C: DARM'S DIMENSIONAL PORTAL (UNDERWORLD ROUTE):
  1. Knock on Darm's door with Mood set to Lying; tell him Brandon summoned
     him to Castle Kyrandia.
  2. Enter storm cellar; pick up 3 Colored Crystals.
  3. Insert crystals into portal ring: Red (Ruby) -> Yellow -> Blue (Sapphire).
  4. Step into the active vortex to teleport directly to the Isle of Cats.

[04.04] ACT IV: THE ROYAL COURT TRIAL & 3 FINAL ENDINGS                 [WLK04]
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

ENDING 1: TRUE EXONERATION (CANONICAL GOOD ENDING):
  1. Immediately toggle Mood Meter to Truthful (v_mood = 2, Blue Angel).
  2. Declare: "I am completely innocent, Your Majesty."
  3. Place Magic Mirror of Truth in center of courtroom.
  4. The mirror reveals Gunther the Demon hiding inside Malcolm's shadow!
  5. Gunther is banished to the void; Malcolm is cleared of all crimes and
     appointed Royal Court Jester of Kyrandia.

ENDING 2: KING MALCOLM (MONARCHIC LAW ENDING):
  1. Set Mood to Normal (v_mood = 1).
  2. Present ancient constitution scroll and Royal Crown of Kyrandia.
  3. Malcolm claims the throne under Article IV; crowned King Malcolm!

ENDING 3: PETRIFICATION VENGEANCE (EVIL ENDING):
  1. Set Mood to Lying (v_mood = 0).
  2. Pull out enchanted Jester Wand; cast petrification spell.
  3. Brandon, Kallak, and jury turn to stone; Malcolm rules supreme!

[06.00] HISTORICAL COPY-PROTECTION & CD-ROM CHECKS                      [PROT]
===============================================================================

A. 1994 OPTICAL MEDIA VERIFICATION (MSCDEX):
  Unlike Book 1 (manual potion lookup) and Book 2 (spellbook glyph prompts),
  Book 3 was released natively on CD-ROM in 1994. The copy protection relied
  on two low-level hardware interrupt checks:
  1. MSCDEX Volume Label: Checked INT 2Fh, AX=1500h to verify drive status
     and confirm the root disc label matched ASCII "KYRA3".
  2. Redbook CD Audio Subchannel Q-Data Check: Verified optical audio tracks.

B. REVERSE-ENGINEERED ASSEMBLY BYPASS:
  Early DOS crackers patched MAIN.EXE by NOPing the CD driver check loop:
    0040:12A7  JZ Loc_NoCD_Fail  ; 0x74 0x22 -> Jump to "Insert CD" error
    Patched to:
    0040:12A7  NOP               ; 0x90
    0040:12A8  NOP               ; 0x90 (Bypasses MSCDEX failure)
    0040:12A9  MOV AX, 0001h     ; Force success return code

A. MASTER ASSET CONTAINER ARCHITECTURE:
  • KYRA3.DAT: Master bundle (Sprite sheets, palette DAC tables, font maps).
  • VOCAB.PAK: Text dialogue scripts and subtitle timing indexes.
  • CHARS.PAK: Character animation state frames and walking directional maps.
  • SOUND.PAK: Digital sound FX samples (22 kHz 8-bit PCM).

B. SILICON GRAPHICS 3D PRE-RENDERED SPRITES:
  Book 3 was one of the earliest PC adventure games to utilize Silicon
  Graphics (SGI) workstations to render 3D character models into 256-color
  VGA 2D sprite sheets, yielding remarkably fluid character animations.
