[03.00] THE BIRTH OF THE RTS AI: HISTORICAL CONTEXT                     [HIST]
===============================================================================

Released in 1992 by Westwood Studios, Dune II defined the modern Real-Time
Strategy (RTS) genre. Running on a 16MHz Intel 286/386 with under 640 KB of
DOS memory, the AI could not use modern neural nets or deep search trees.
Westwood built an ultra-efficient suite of Finite State Machines (FSMs) and
heuristic weight tables that simulated a live opponent in real time.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[04.01] BASE CONSTRUCTION & SCRIPT PLACEMENT LOGIC                      [ARC01]
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. PREDEFINED SCRIPT ANCHORS:
   The AI does not dynamically survey rock plateaus. Building locations are
   pre-anchored in SCENARIO.PAK map tables.
2. RECONSTRUCTION LOOP (t_rebuild):
   When a player destroys an AI building, an internal timer fires. If the
   Construction Yard remains active, the AI automatically queues a replacement.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[04.02] ECONOMIC REPLENISHMENT & HARVESTER ROUTING                      [ARC02]
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

• Spice Harvesting: Harvesters seek spice within a 16-tile taxicab radius.
• Carryall Priority: When an AI Harvester fills to 100%, the engine gives it
  top priority over player harvesters for Carryall airlift.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[04.03] WAVE ASSEMBLY & STAGING TRIGGER FSM                             [ARC03]
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The AI pools manufactured units at a designated staging rally point. Only when
PoolCount >= MaxThreshold does the group switch into ATTACK_STATE.

[05.00] HOUSE-SPECIFIC BEHAVIORAL BIASES & TACTICS                      [HOUS]
===============================================================================

1. HOUSE ATREIDES (DEFENSIVE TACTICIAN):
   • Moderate attack wave frequency; high armor unit composition.
   • Sonic Tanks fire straight-line soundwaves damaging everything in path.
   • Fremen infantry spawn from Palace and march autonomously on player base.

2. HOUSE HARKONNEN (HEAVY SIEGE JUGGERNAUT):
   • Relentless assault waves with Siege Tanks and Devastator mechs.
   • Devastators overload reactors on death, annihilating nearby units.
   • Death Hand nuke missile targets player Construction Yard (±4 tile drift).

3. HOUSE ORDOS (HIT-AND-RUN MERCENARIES):
   • Fast Raider Trikes for economic raids and Harvester sniping.
   • Deviator nerve gas converts player tanks to Ordos control for 15 seconds.
   • Saboteur stealth assassins infiltrate player buildings for suicide blasts.

===============================================================================

SANDWORM HUNTING & NOISE SENSING:
  1. Noise Register: Soft sand travel adds noise (Infantry +1, Tanks +8).
  2. Stalking: Sandworm vectors directly toward the highest noise epicenter.
  3. Strike & Satiation: Devours target tile; despawns after eating 3 vehicles.

===============================================================================
[07.00] AI BLINDSPOTS, PATHFINDING GLITCHES & EXPLOITS                  [EXPL]
===============================================================================

• Rocket Turret Outranging: Turrets hit at 6 tiles; AI aggro sensors only see
  5 tiles away, allowing un-retaliated destruction of incoming forces.
• Cliff Wall Sliding: AI pathfinding oscillates indefinitely when blocked by
  concrete slabs or walls on diagonal cliff edges.
• Sandworm Baiting: Use a cheap Trike to bait enemy tanks onto deep sand where
  Sandworms consume them.
