The Mood Meter Engine [BASE]

Malcolm's interactions depend directly on the 3-state Mood Meter register (v_mood):

v_mood = 0
Lying / Sarcastic
Tricks guards & bluffs Darm
v_mood = 1
Normal / Neutral
Standard item manipulation
v_mood = 2
Truthful / Polite

Act I: Escape from Kyrandia [WLK01]

Select your escape route from petrified exile:

  • Route A (Pegasus): Inflate float with Bellows (4 clicks); combine berry juice + leather glove for polish; fly to Isle of Cats.
  • Route B (Pirate Ship): Bluff Bank teller for 50 Gold Sovereigns; deliver Sea Chart to Captain Jean-Claude.
  • Route C (Portal): Lie to Darm; align Red, Yellow, and Blue crystals in storm cellar portal ring.

Act II: The Isle of Cats & Colosseum [WLK02]

  • Weave reeds into basket; bait with Swiss cheese; capture Jungle Mouse.
  • Bribe prison guard dog with bone; liberate mice to earn the Mouse Flute.
  • Extract Sesame Oil at Colosseum press; lubricate gate; rout Champion with Mouse Flute.
  • Claim Colosseum Trophy; drop into Underworld chasm.

Act III: The Underworld & Queen of the Dead [WLK03]

  • Combine Bone Hook + Spirit Worm; fish Glowing Lost Soul from Lava River.
  • Pay Charon the Soul; sail across magma sea to Queen Levitia's Palace.
  • Solve scale riddle (feather vs lead); claim Royal Crown & Magic Mirror of Truth.
  • Step into ascension vortex to surface inside Castle Kyrandia courtroom.

Historical Copy-Protection & CD-ROM Checks [PROT]

In 1994, Westwood implemented optical subchannel verification via MSCDEX (Interrupt INT 2Fh, AX=1500h). Early crackers NOPed the driver check loop:

; Original Westwood MSCDEX Check (MAIN.EXE)
0040:12A7  JZ Loc_NoCD_Fail   ; Jump to error if MSCDEX absent
0040:12A9  CALL CheckDiscLabel

; Reverse-Engineered Hard Drive Patch
0040:12A7  NOP                ; 0x90
0040:12A8  NOP                ; 0x90 (Bypasses check)
0040:12A9  MOV AX, 0001h      ; Return success
