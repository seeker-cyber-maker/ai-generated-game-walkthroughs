[06.00] THE GREAT MUSEUM OF THE ANCIENTS: EXHIBITS & SECRETS            [MUSM]
===============================================================================

The Great Museum in Disc 4 is one of the most famous locations in Westwood RPG
history, containing critical puzzle machinery and interactive exhibits:

A. GALLERY 1 (THE PREHISTORIC HALL):
  • Giant fossilized beast skeletons and ancient automatons.
  • Align the 3 dynamo coils to charge the Empty Power Cell into a Charged
    Power Cell (+100 energy).

B. GALLERY 2 (THE HALL OF RELICS & LORE):
  • Wax likenesses of Scotia, King Richard, The Draracle, and the Nether Mask.
  • Inscriptions explain that Scotia's Nether Mask was an ancient bio-filter
    created by Belial's disciples.
  • Place 4 elemental stones on pedestals to lower force field and claim the
    Prismatic Gauntlet!

C. GALLERY 3 (SECRET WESTWOOD DEVELOPER MUSEUM / EASTER EGG):
  • Press anomalous bronze rivet at coordinate (X:14, Y:09) on 2nd floor.
  • Opens secret gallery with digitized portraits of the Westwood development
    team (Rick Gush, Louis Castle, Philip Gorrow), sound test terminals, and
    early prototype character concept sketches!
