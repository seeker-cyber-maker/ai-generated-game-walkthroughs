-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[05.01] SCREENS 01-05: COUNTRYSIDE & CASTLE                             [WLK01]
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

• Screen 01 (VCTH): Hooter punches apple tree. BoBo casts magic on horn on
  rock to enlarge it. Dwayne picks up diamond from ground; walk east.
• Screen 02 (ECOP): BoBo casts spell on cloud to make it rain; beanstalk
  grows. Dwayne collects jug. Hooter climbs beanstalk.
• Screen 03 (FTST): Hooter punches skeleton for bone. Dwayne feeds bone to
  guard dog. BoBo levitates key to Dwayne.
• Screen 04 (TKNK): Dwayne mines crystal with pickaxe. BoBo illuminates
  crystal. Hooter climbs pillar.
• Screen 05 (SLVR): BoBo grows mushroom into trampoline. Hooter bounces to
  upper ledge to pull switch.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[05.02] SCREENS 06-10: CAVERNS & UNDERGROUND LAKE                       [WLK02]
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

• Screen 06 (PNCV): BoBo enchants spider web into safety net. Hooter drops
  stalactite. Dwayne collects lantern.
• Screen 07 (HVRT): Dwayne places plank on river. BoBo enchants sail with
  wind to cross subterranean lake.
• Screen 08 (BSTR): Dwayne throws meat to distract sleeping dragon. Hooter
  slips past and pulls wall lever.
• Screen 09 (DRGN): BoBo enlarges giant ear trumpet. Hooter rings gong.
• Screen 10 (FGLR): Dwayne uncorks wine bottle; BoBo drops pepper on meat;
  Hooter moves bread loaf.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[05.03] SCREENS 11-15: GIANT'S TABLE & TREE HOLLOWS                     [WLK03]
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

• Screen 11 (MTRC): Load carrot into toy cannon; fire at target.
• Screen 12 (RNGR): BoBo enchants woodpecker to hollow tree; Dwayne gets acorn.
• Screen 13 (CRWN): Dwayne mixes potion flasks; BoBo casts transmutation spell.
• Screen 14 (PLNT): Feed fly to plant; Hooter crosses vine bridge safely.
• Screen 15 (WTRF): Dwayne jams windmill; BoBo freezes waterfall staircase.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[05.04] SCREENS 16-20: DARK WIZARD CASTLE                               [WLK04]
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

• Screen 16 (GBLN): Dwayne cuts drawbridge rope with shears.
• Screen 17 (STRM): BoBo animates spellbook; Hooter hits bookcase lever.
• Screen 18 (CLCK): Dwayne sets clock to 12:00; BoBo stops pendulum.
• Screen 19 (SHDW): Reflect lightning bolt with handheld mirror.
• Screen 20 (FRST): Dwayne unlocks dungeon cell door; liberate court jester.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[05.05] SCREENS 21-22: VOODOO DOLL & KING'S CURE                        [WLK05]
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

• Screen 21 (VDDO): Hooter climbs voodoo doll arm; extracts needle from chest.
  BoBo turns needle into golden feather; Dwayne collects feather.
• Screen 22 (ENDG): Dwayne tickles wizard's nose with feather to make him
  sneeze; wizard drops wand. BoBo casts magic to shatter wand! King is cured!

[07.00] HISTORICAL COPY-PROTECTION & COKTEL DRM BYPASSES                [PROT]
===============================================================================

A. THE 1991 MANUAL GLYPH & RUNE MATRIX:
  In 1991, Coktel Vision protected Gobliiins with a printed manual lookup.
  Before accessing screens 6, 11, and 16, the game displayed a wizard rune
  asking the player to enter coordinates from page 4 of the manual.

B. REVERSE-ENGINEERED TOT SCRIPT CRACK:
  In EMAJ1001.TOT, the engine opcode handler evaluated the rune input:
    EVAL_INPUT [user_rune] == [expected_rune]
    IF_FALSE JUMP Screen_Failed_Energy_Drain
  Early crackers patched the conditional jump to unconditionally proceed.
