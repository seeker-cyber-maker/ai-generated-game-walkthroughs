[03.00] GAME BASICS, CONTROLS & PARSER MECHANICS                        [BASE]
===============================================================================

A. INTERFACE & CONTROLS:
  • Arrow Keys / Number Pad: Move Roger Wilco in 8 directions.
  • Left Mouse Click       : Walk directly to clicked coordinate.
  • Tab                    : Open inventory window.
  • F2                     : Toggle sound and Roland MT-32 music.
  • F3                     : Recall last typed command into parser input line.
  • F5 / F7                : Save Game / Restore Game.
  • Spacebar               : Dismiss on-screen message text boxes.

===============================================================================

A. THE SCUMSOFT CARDBOARD KEYCARD GRID:
  In 1989, Sierra shipped Space Quest III with a dark gray anti-photocopy
  cardboard sheet containing alien symbol grids. Entering ScumSoft required
  matching row/column symbols against the prompt on the terminal.

B. ASTRO CHICKEN CAESAR SUBSTITUTION CIPHER:
  The arcade cabinet transmission used a Rot-26 inversion cipher:
    Cipher : URSN! ZH ZIV YVRMT SVOW XZKGREV YB FXFNHLUG...
    Decoded: HELP! WE ARE BEING HELD CAPTIVE BY SCUMSOFT ON PESTULON!

C. REVERSE-ENGINEERED SCI0 BYTECODE CRACK (LOGIC.044):
  In LOGIC.044, Sierra's PMachine compares symbol inputs:
    pushi v_entered_symbol
    pushi v_expected_symbol
    eq?
    bnt RejectAccess  ->  Patched to NOP NOP to force access approval.
