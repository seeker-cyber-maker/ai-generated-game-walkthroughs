2. Complete Step-by-Step Walkthrough [WLK00]

Chapter 1: Swamps of Kyrandia & Ferry Crossing [WLK01]

Area Item Checklist: Chapter 1
  1. Take the Flasks and Alchemy Book. Brew the Potion of Skepticism (Water + Onion + Vinegar).
  2. Drink the Skeptic Potion to dispel the ghostly illusion at the dock. Pay the ferryman with the Gold Coin.

Chapter 2: Morningmist Valley & Dragon Rider [WLK02]

Area Item Checklist: Chapter 2
  1. Brew the Sandwich Potion to bribe the hungry dragon guard.
  2. Equip the young dragon with the Saddle to fly to the Volcanic Core.

Chapter 3: The Volcanic Core & Dragon Caverns [WLK03]

Area Item Checklist: Chapter 3
  1. Brew the Dragon Breath Potion to blast open the sealed rock tunnel.
  2. Power the tram with the Gold Ingot to ascend into the Cloud Highlands.

Chapter 4 & 5: Cloud Highlands & The Hand of Fate [WLK04/WLK05]

Area Item Checklist: Chapters 4 & 5
  1. Mix the Snowman Potion before the Snowball melts! Drink to bypass the heat barrier.
  2. Drink the Pegasus Potion to glide into the Center of the World.
  3. The Hand of Fate Battle:
    • Place the Rainbow Prism on the floor to lure the Giant Hand.
    • Pull the brake lever to crush its fingers in the giant gears and save Kyrandia!

5. Engine Forensics: The Cauldron Accumulator [ENGN]

How KYRA2.EXE evaluates alchemy bitmasks inside ALCHEMY.PAK:

Cauldron Reagent Bitfield:
Dropping Reagent -> g_cauldron_mask |= (1 << ITEM_ID)
Using Empty Flask -> LookupRecipeTable(g_cauldron_mask)
Matching mask -> Yields Potion ID; invalid mask -> Toxic Sludge (0xFF).
