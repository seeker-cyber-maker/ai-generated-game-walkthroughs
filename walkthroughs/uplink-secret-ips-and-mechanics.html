The Story of Introversion: Bedroom Programmers & The Developer CD [HIST]

1. The Kitchen Table Factory (2001)

Four Imperial College London grads (Chris Delay, Mark Morris, John Austin, Thomas Arundel) hand-burned CD-Rs, folded black cardboard boxes on their kitchen table, and carried sacks of mail to the post office every day.

2. The Strategy First Betrayal (2003)

US publisher Strategy First shipped tens of thousands of copies into US retail stores, entered Canadian bankruptcy protection (CCAA), and never paid royalties, leaving Introversion broke and facing liquidation.

3. Selling the C++ Source Code to Survive: The Developer CD

To avoid selling their IP to a conglomerate, Introversion made an unprecedented gamble: selling the full, raw commercial C++ source code of Uplink on the Developer CD for £30. It saved the studio, financed Darwinia & DEFCON, and launched one of the most vibrant modding communities in PC history!
