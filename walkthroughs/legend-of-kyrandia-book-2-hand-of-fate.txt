While this document was assembled and mathematically verified with the
assistance of modern AI tooling and binary disassembly pipelines, it is born
from a deep, lifelong love for classic gaming. The author spent countless
cherished hours playing, mapping, and loving these games as a kid, teenager,
adult, and even yesterday.

While traditional narrative walkthroughs and secrets have been known for
decades, approaching them today from the perspective of an AI Cybersecurity
Researcher and Reverse-Engineer offers a new dimension of appreciation. By
peering directly beneath the hood into decompiled assembly, memory
registers, and state-machine bytecode, we can finally understand--
deterministically and mathematically--what made these cherished old games
tick, every single tick.

[04.00] GAME BASICS & THE ALCHEMY CAULDRON                              [BASE]
===============================================================================

In The Legend of Kyrandia: Book 2 - Hand of Fate, you play as the Royal
Alchemist Zanthia. The core mechanic revolves around combining alchemical
reagents into your portable cauldron or flask to produce mandatory magical
potions.

  (approx 60 real-world seconds). Gather reagents BEFORE plucking the snow!

  the gust of wind locks the door permanently.

[09.00] ENGINE FORENSICS: THE CAULDRON ACCUMULATOR                      [ENGN]
===============================================================================

Westwood's ALCHEMY.PAK evaluates ingredients via a 32-bit bitfield mask:

  Ingredient Mask:
    Bit 0 (0x01): Water Flask
    Bit 1 (0x02): Fireberries
    Bit 2 (0x04): Swamp Onion
    Bit 3 (0x08): Mustard Seed

  Evaluation Rule:
    Current_Cauldron_Mask |= Item_Bitmask;
    if (Current_Cauldron_Mask == TARGET_POTION_MASK) -> Emit_Potion();

This architecture allows ingredients to be dropped in ANY order without
failing the recipe!
