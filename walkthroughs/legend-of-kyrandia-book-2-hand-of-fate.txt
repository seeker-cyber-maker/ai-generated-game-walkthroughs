[03.00] GAME BASICS & THE ALCHEMY CAULDRON                              [BASE]
===============================================================================

Zanthia the Alchemist carries a portable Cauldron and an Alchemy Recipe Book.
Dropping ingredients into the cauldron sets bitmask flags. Using an empty
flask on the cauldron brews the potion.

VOLATILE ITEM TIMER:
Snowballs and ice cubes melt into empty water puddles after 1,200 engine ticks
(~120 seconds). Always gather non-volatile reagents first before picking snow!

1. Zanthia's Plundered Hut: Take the Flasks and Alchemy Recipe Book. Fill a

2. Retrieve Onion & Vinegar: Collect the Onion from the vegetable patch and

3. Brew Skeptic Potion: Mix Water + Onion + Vinegar in the cauldron. Fill an

4. Ferry Crossing: Drink the Skeptic Potion to dispel the ghostly ferryman's
   illusion. Pay him the Gold Coin to cross to Morningmist.

===============================================================================

For speedrunners and fast replays. Execute strictly these 15 steps:

STEP 01: [Zanthia Hut] ───────► Take Flasks & Recipe -> Fill flask with water.
STEP 02: [Swamp Veg Patch] ───► Pick Onion & sour berries -> Brew Vinegar.
STEP 03: [Cauldron Room] ─────► Mix Water + Onion + Vinegar -> Skeptic Potion.
STEP 04: [Ferry Dock] ────────► Drink Skeptic Potion -> Pay Coin -> Ride Ferry.
STEP 05: [Farmer Valley] ─────► Harvest Wheat -> Grind at windmill for Dough.
STEP 06: [Dairy Cellar] ──────► Take Cheese & Mustard -> Brew Sandwich Potion.
STEP 07: [Dragon Pen] ────────► Give Sandwich to guard -> Equip Saddle -> Fly.
STEP 08: [Lava Core] ─────────► Take Sulphur & Pepper -> Brew Dragon Breath.
STEP 09: [Sealed Tunnel] ─────► Blast rocks -> Power Tram with Gold Ingot.
STEP 10: [Snow Peak] ─────────► Mix Snowball + Twigs + Coal before snow melts!
STEP 11: [Thermal Gate] ──────► Drink Snowman Potion -> Pass guard into Clouds.
STEP 12: [Cloud Funnel] ──────► Mix Feather + Star + Water -> Drink Pegasus.
STEP 13: [World Machine] ─────► Insert Gear Key into central core -> Align.
STEP 14: [Arena] ─────────────► Drop Rainbow Prism on floor to lure Giant Hand.
STEP 15: [Brake Lever] ───────► Pull lever to crush Hand in gears -> WIN!

[08.00] ENGINE FORENSICS: THE CAULDRON ACCUMULATOR                      [ENGN]
===============================================================================

Westwood's KYRA2.EXE engine evaluates alchemy via an unordered bitmask inside
ALCHEMY.PAK:
  Dropping an item sets: g_cauldron_mask |= (1 << ITEM_ID)
  Using a flask triggers: LookupRecipeTable(g_cauldron_mask)
  Matching mask yields Potion ID; invalid mask yields green toxic sludge.
