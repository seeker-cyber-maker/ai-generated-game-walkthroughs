While this document was assembled and mathematically verified with the
assistance of modern AI tooling and binary disassembly pipelines, it is born
from a deep, lifelong love for classic gaming. The author spent countless
cherished hours playing, mapping, and loving these games as a kid, teenager,
adult, and even yesterday.

While traditional narrative walkthroughs and secrets have been known for
decades, approaching them today from the perspective of an AI Cybersecurity

===============================================================================
[04.01] ACT I: SWAMP HUT & POTION OF SKEPTICISM                         [WLK01]
===============================================================================

1. Hut: Take Empty Flasks and Recipe Book from the stand. Fill one flask with
   swamp water outside.
2. Garden: Harvest an Onion from the vegetable patch and Sour Berries from the
   thorn bush.
3. Still: Place berries in copper still to distill pure Vinegar.
4. Cauldron: Add Water + Onion + Vinegar into cauldron. Dip empty flask to
   obtain the Potion of Skepticism.
5. Ferry: Drink Skepticism potion to dispel illusion. Pay Gold Coin to board.

===============================================================================
[04.02] ACT II: MORNING MIST VALLEY & DRAGON FLIGHT                     [WLK02]
===============================================================================

1. Valley: Harvest ripe Wheat from field. Grind at windmill for Dough.
2. Dairy Cellar: Collect aged Cheese and spicy Mustard seeds.
3. Cauldron: Brew Sandwich Potion (Dough + Mustard + Cheese).
4. Dragon Pen: Feed sandwich to hungry dragon guard. Equip Dragon Saddle and
   take flight to volcanic mountains!

===============================================================================
[04.03] ACT III: VOLCANIC CORE & LAVA CAVERNS                           [WLK03]
===============================================================================

1. Lava Core: Collect Sulphur Stone from vent and Hot Pepper from bush.
2. Cauldron: Brew Dragon Breath Potion (Sulphur + Pepper + Water).
3. Sealed Tunnel: Drink potion to breathe fire and blast rock wall. Power ore
   tram with Gold Ingot and ride into highlands.

===============================================================================
[04.04] ACT IV: CLOUD HEIGHTS & SNOWMAN POTION                          [WLK04]
===============================================================================

1. Snow Peak: Gather Charcoal and Dry Twigs first. Pick Snowball last!
2. Cauldron: Rapidly brew Snowman Potion (Snowball + Twigs + Charcoal) before
   the snowball melts.
3. Drink potion for thermal immunity and slip past storm guard into Cloud
   Heights.

===============================================================================
[04.05] ACT V: THE WORLD MACHINE & GEAR CORE                            [WLK05]
===============================================================================

1. Cloud Funnel: Brew Pegasus Flight Potion (Feather + Starfish + Water).
2. Glide down funnel into ancient World Machine sanctum.
3. Align elemental cogs and insert Gear Key into central shaft.

===============================================================================
[04.06] ACT VI: CENTER OF THE WORLD & FINAL SHOWDOWN                    [WLK06]
===============================================================================

1. Arena: Drop Rainbow Prism on center tile to lure the Giant Hand.
2. When the Hand lunges for the prism, pull heavy brake lever!
3. The gears crush the Hand, restoring Anchor Stone and saving Kyrandia!

[07.00] ENGINE FORENSICS: THE CAULDRON STATE-MACHINE                    [ENGN]
===============================================================================

Westwood's KYRA2.EXE engine manages alchemy via an unordered bitfield
ingredient accumulator inside ALCHEMY.PAK:
  • Item Bitmask: Dropping item sets: g_cauldron_mask |= (1 << ITEM_ID)
  • Recipe Lookup: When flask is used, evaluates LookupRecipeTable(g_mask).
  • Validation: If valid, dispenses potion; if invalid, flushes green smoke.

[08.00] HISTORICAL COPY-PROTECTION & X86 BINARY CRACKING                [CRCK]
===============================================================================

A. RETAIL COPY-PROTECTION MECHANISM:
  At Darm's house, the game prompted player for specific ingredient words or
  page numbers from the retail manual (e.g., "DARM", "BRANDY", "FEATHER").

B. X86 DISASSEMBLY & PATCH VECTORS:
  In KYRA2.EXE / MAIN.EXE, protection check executed via:
    CALL check_spellbook_protection
    TEST AX, AX
    JZ   fail_protection_trap    ; Opcode: 74 1A
  Historical crackers bypassed this check using two methods:
  1. Forcing Jump: Changing JZ (0x74 0x1A) to NOPs (0x90 0x90) so execution
     always falls through to success branch.
  2. Subroutine Bypass: Overwrite CALL check with MOV AX, 0001h (0xB8 0x01)
     followed by RET (0xC3).

[09.00] SEQUEL BRIDGES, CHARACTER EVOLUTION & MALCOLM'S REVENGE         [SEQL]
===============================================================================

A. ZANTHIA'S CHARACTER ARC & THE DISAPPEARING REALM:
  "Hand of Fate" elevates Zanthia from supporting mystic into the quick-witted,
  pragmatic lead of the trilogy. Her quest reveals that the disappearing land
  was orchestrated by the Giant Hand (Marko's rogue glove animated by cosmic
  magic), setting up the physical restoration of Kyrandia.

B. MARKO & THE CONTINUITY TO MALCOLM'S REVENGE (BOOK 3):
  The bungling magician Marko, who assists Zanthia in Book 2, returns in "The
  Legend of Kyrandia: Book 3 - Malcolm's Revenge" (1994). The political fallout
  from Book 2's near-catastrophe leads directly to the royal court's suspicion
  and the high-stakes trial of Malcolm.
