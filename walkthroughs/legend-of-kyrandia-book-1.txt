[01.00] AUTHOR'S PREFACE & RESEARCH PHILOSOPHY                          [PREF]
===============================================================================

While this document was assembled and mathematically verified with the
assistance of modern AI tooling and binary disassembly pipelines, it is born
from a deep, lifelong love for classic gaming. The author spent countless
cherished hours playing, mapping, and loving these games as a kid, teenager,
adult, and even yesterday.

While traditional narrative walkthroughs and secrets have been known for
decades, approaching them today from the perspective of an AI Cybersecurity
Researcher and Reverse-Engineer offers a new dimension of appreciation. By
peering directly beneath the hood into decompiled assembly, memory
registers, and state-machine bytecode, we can finally understand
(deterministically and mathematically) what made these cherished old games
tick, every single tick.

[04.00] COMPLETE STEP-BY-STEP WALKTHROUGH                               [WLK00]
===============================================================================

===============================================================================
[04.01] ACT I: TIMBERMIST WOODS & BRANDON'S AWAKENING                   [WLK01]
===============================================================================

1. Grandfather's Hut: Witness Kallak petrified by Malcolm. Pick up the Letter
   from the desk and Apple from the floor.
2. Timbermist Woods: Meet Brynn in her forest hut. Give her the Rose to charge
   the Tear of Healing on your amulet (Yellow Gem).
3. Hermit's Shack: Collect the Saw and fix the wooden bridge.

===============================================================================
[04.02] ACT II: THE GREAT TREE & SERPENT'S GROTTO                       [WLK02]
===============================================================================

1. Great Tree: Cast Healing on the withered trunk to produce fresh Lavender.
2. Darm's Hut: Trade Lavender with Darm for the Magic Quill.
3. Serpent's Grotto: Retrieve the Iron Key and Fireberry Flask.

===============================================================================
[04.03] ACT III: CAVERNS OF TWILIGHT & FIREFLY MAZE                     [WLK03]
===============================================================================

1. Cavern Entrance: Catch three glowing Fireflies in the Fireberry Flask.
2. Maze Navigation: Travel through the 28 rooms, lighting Fireberry Bushes
   before your carried berries exhaust their 3-step charge!
3. Royal Tomb: Cast Healing on King William's spirit to gain the Royal Scepter.

===============================================================================
[04.04] ACT IV: ZANTHIA'S REALM & ROYAL ALCHEMY LAB                     [WLK04]
===============================================================================

1. Swamp Realm: Meet Zanthia the Royal Mystic in her laboratory.
2. Potion Brewing: Mix Hot Water + Dragon Tears to brew Levitation Potion.
3. Castle Moat: Drink potion to float across the chasm into Malcolm's Castle.

===============================================================================
[04.05] ACT V: MALCOLM'S CASTLE & KYRAGEM FINALE                        [WLK05]
===============================================================================

1. Great Hall: Solve the Crown, Scepter, and Royal Ring pedestal puzzle.
2. Kyragem Chamber: Cast Invisibility (Blue Gem) to sneak past Malcolm's gaze.
3. The Mirror: Position the Magic Mirror behind the Kyragem. Malcolm's dark
   magic bounces back, turning him to solid stone! Brandon is crowned King!

[06.00] IN-DEPTH SYSTEMS COMPENDIUM                                     [COMP]
===============================================================================

A. THE KYRAGEM AMULET (4 MAGICAL DISCIPLINES):
  • Yellow Gem (Healing)      : Awakens dead trees and revives spirits.
  • Purple Gem (Poison/Wisp)  : Transforms Brandon into a glowing wisp.
  • Blue Gem (Invisibility)   : Masks presence from Malcolm and guard beasts.
  • Orange Gem (Fire / Magic) : Discharges bursts of mystical energy.

B. FIREBERRY VOLATILITY MECHANICS:
  Fireberries expire after exactly 3 screen transitions (v_berry_charge = 3).
  Stepping into a dark room with 0 charge triggers the lethal Shadow Monster!

DECOMPILED KYRA1.EXE BIRTHSTONE SEED FORMULA:
  GemIndex = (Seed * 0x41C64E6D + 0x3039) >> 16
  TargetGemSlot2 = GemIndex % 10

PALETTE CYCLING REGISTERS:
  VGA DAC registers 0x3C8/0x3C9 are rotated at 60 Hz to animate glowing
  fireflies and swirling alchemical cauldrons without CPU redraw overhead.

[08.00] SEQUEL BRIDGES, CHARACTER EVOLUTION & KYRANDIA TRILOGY         [SEQL]
===============================================================================

A. BRANDON: FROM ORPHAN WOODSMAN TO MONARCH:
  Brandon begins as a naive peasant boy, discovering his royal heritage as King
  William's heir. In "Book 2: Hand of Fate", Brandon rules Kyrandia alongside
  the Mystics, and in "Book 3: Malcolm's Revenge", he presides as the severe
  prosecutor during Malcolm's trial.

B. MALCOLM: THE COMPLEX JESTER & REDEMPTION IN BOOK 3:
  In Book 1, Malcolm appears as a purely sadistic jester who petrifies Kallak.
  However, in "Book 3: Malcolm's Revenge" (1994), Malcolm escapes his stone
  prison and sets out to prove his innocence, revealing that the royal murders
  were actually committed by the malevolent spirit Gunther!

C. ZANTHIA: FROM SUPPORTING ALCHEMIST TO SOLO HEROINE IN BOOK 2:
  Zanthia transitions from Brandon's potion mentor in Book 1 into the spunky,
  witty protagonist of "The Legend of Kyrandia: Hand of Fate" (1993), venturing
  to the center of the world to save the realm from disappearing.

D. PREQUEL-TO-SEQUEL ENGINE & INTERFACE EVOLUTION MATRIX:
