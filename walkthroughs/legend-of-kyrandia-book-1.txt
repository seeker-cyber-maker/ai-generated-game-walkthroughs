[01.00] AUTHOR'S PREFACE & RESEARCH PHILOSOPHY                          [PREF]
===============================================================================

While this document was assembled and mathematically verified with the
assistance of modern AI tooling and binary disassembly pipelines, it is born
from a deep, lifelong love for classic gaming. The author spent countless
cherished hours playing, mapping, and loving these games as a kid, teenager,
adult, and even yesterday.

While traditional narrative walkthroughs and secrets have been known for
decades, approaching them today from the perspective of an AI Researcher,
Software Developer, and AI Cybersecurity Reverse-Engineer offers a new
dimension of appreciation. By peering directly beneath the hood into
decompiled assembly, memory registers, and state-machine bytecode, we can
finally understand--deterministically and mathematically--what made these
cherished old games tick, every single tick.

[07.00] ENGINE FORENSICS & BINARY REVERSE-ENGINEERING                   [ENGN]
===============================================================================

A. 4-SLOT BIRTHSTONE ALTAR ENGINE (KYRA.EXE):
  Slot 1: Sunstone (100% hardcoded invariable).
  Slot 2: Random Gem A (Chosen at session start: Seed % 10).
  Slot 3: Random Gem B (Chosen at session start: (Seed + 3) % 10).
  Slot 4: Ruby (100% hardcoded invariable).

B. 16-ROOM OUTDOOR RESPAWN POOL:
  Rejected items teleport to: Forest_Respawn_Vector[NextSeed % 16].
  Items are never destroyed or lost permanently.

C. MAZE TOPOLOGY & RNG ILLUSION VS INDIANA JONES (SCUMM):
  • Kyrandia 1: 28-room static directed graph using 5 reused .CPS background
    art tiles and a 3-step fireberry volatility counter (v_berry_charge = 3).
  • Fate of Atlantis: SCUMM v5 dynamically seeds Plato's 3 stone dials
    (Sun/Moon/World) and picks target items from a 4-room candidate pool,
    branching into 3 distinct paths (Wits, Fists, Team).
