[04.00] GAME BASICS, COMPANION ROLES & THE 30-DAY CLOCK                 [BASE]
===============================================================================

+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| THE THREE SACRED GRAINS OF SAND PROPHECY                                    |
|                                                                             |
| In the mystical Blue Land, the wicked sorcerer Kraal will unleash eternal   |
| darkness on the 30th day. Only Ween, guided by Petroy, can gather the       |
| 3 Grains of Sand and activate the Great Hourglass of Revuss in time!        |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------+

A. THE 30-DAY HARD TIME LIMIT:
  Every screen transition and rested night advances the internal clock. If Day
  30 arrives before placing all 3 Grains into the Great Hourglass, Kraal
  captures the kingdom, triggering a permanent game over (PERDUFX).

B. COMPANION SPECIALIZATION MATRIX:
  • Ween (Protagonist): Apprentice wizard. Combines reagents, casts spells
    (Morphosys, Luciferys, Vitalys), and mixes items in the haversack.
  • Petroy (Mentor): Squeaks acoustic soundwaves, manipulates magical conduits.
  • Orain (Warrior): Lifts heavy stone blocks, operates forge bellows.
  • Urm (Trickster): Climbs trees, fits into tight crevices, steals items.

===============================================================================
[06.01] ACT I: COTTAGE PORCH, FOREST & FIRST GRAIN                      [WLK01]
===============================================================================

1. Screen 1 (Cottage Porch & Yard - EMAJ1000):
   - Gather loose Straw from the broom on the porch.
   - Collect Wooden Sticks leaning against the woodpile under the eaves.
   - Pick fresh Wild Strawberries along the dirt garden path.
   - Use a Wooden Stick on the roof gutter to dislodge the bronze coin.
   - Combine Stick + Straw + String in inventory to make a makeshift tool.
   - Feed a Strawberry to the creature near the doorstep to pacify it.
   - Unlatch the front door using the tool and step inside.

2. Screen 2 (Laboratory, Cellar & Strawberry Jam - EMAJ1001/1002):
   - Grab Brass Key from mantle, Mortar & Pestle from desk.
   - Cook strawberries in copper pot over hearth fire -> Strawberry Jam.
   - Unlock cellar; take Flask, Dried Mandrake Root, and Sulfur Powder.

3. Screen 3 (Forest Crossroads & Shinbone Spear - EMAJ1003/1004):
   - Take the ancient Shinbone (Tibia) from the cairn.
   - Combine Tibia + Small Knife to fashion the sharp Tibia Spear.
   - Send Urm up the hollow oak tree to retrieve the Woodpecker Feather.
   - Slice pine bark to gather sticky Amber Resin.
   - Grind Mandrake + Sulfur in Mortar -> Awakening Dust.

4. Screen 4 (Stone Golem Orgol's Shrine - EMAJ1005/1006):
   - Blow Awakening Dust across the Stone Golem's eyes.
   - Orgol awakens and unlocks the secret carved chest.
   - Claim the First Sacred Grain of Sand (Golden Grain)!

===============================================================================
[06.02] ACT II: ANT MOUND, QUEEN OF ANTS & SECOND GRAIN                 [WLK02]
===============================================================================

1. Screen 5 (Ant Mound & Queen of Ants - EMAJ1023 / BORG1FX):
   - Orgol advises: "Talk to the king of the ants." Enter ant mound.
   - Deploy Mongoose to drive off guard snakes.
   - Offer sweet Strawberry Jam to the Ant Queen (Reine).
   - Worker ants clear the blocked earthen tunnel to let Ween pass.

2. Screen 6 (Bat Cavern & Crystal Prism - EMAJ1009/1011):
   - Command Petroy to squeak; echo scatters bat swarm.
   - Scrape fresh Bat Guano from cavern floor.
   - Mount Quartz Prism on pillar to reflect sunbeam across chasm bridge.

3. Screen 7 (Smelting Forge & Dragon Key - EMAJ1013/1014):
   - Have Orain pump forge bellows.
   - Melt gold bullion in crucible -> Molten Gold.
   - Pour into Wooden Mold; quench in spring water -> Ornate Dragon Key.

4. Screen 8 (Dragon's Lair & Second Grain - EMAJ1015/1017):
   - Throw sticky Amber Resin and Strawberries onto ledge to distract dragon.
   - Unlock obsidian pedestal with Dragon Key -> GET SECOND GRAIN (SILVER)!

===============================================================================
[06.03] ACT III: SICK WORM, CITADEL & HOURGLASS OF REVUSS               [WLK03]
===============================================================================

1. Screen 9 (Giant Worm & Chamomile Herbal Tea - EMAJ1031 / VER1FX):
   - The Giant Worm (Ver) is writhing with a severe stomach ache.
   - Boil spring water in cauldron over hearth fire.
   - Add Chamomile Flowers (Camomille) and Blueberries (Myrtilles) -> Tea.
   - Feed Chamomile Tea to worm; cured of pain, worm yields the jewel key!

2. Screen 10 (Citadel Moat, Glue & Firefly Twig Probe - EMAJ1026/1034):
   - Pour Caustic Acid over rusted drawbridge chains.
   - Catch a glowing Firefly (Luciole) in undergrowth.
   - Apply Glue (Glu) to dry Twig (Brindille); attach Firefly -> Probe.
   - Insert glowing probe into wall slit for Urm to drop drawbridge.

3. Screen 11 (Kraal's Laboratory - EMAJ1025/1030):
   - Take Mirror of Reflection from wall rack; cast Luciferys spell.
   - Reflect Kraal's dark bolt back at him to shatter his shield.
   - Unlock Kraal's vault -> GET THIRD GRAIN (CRYSTAL)!

4. Screen 12 (Hourglass Tower & Finale - EMAJ1035/1036):
   - Ascend pinnacle before Day 30 expires. Solve pendulum rope puzzle.
   - Insert all 3 Grains into Hourglass of Revuss (Gold, Silver, Crystal).
   - Turn the celestial wheel to trap Kraal in a time loop forever (GAGNEFX)!

[09.00] BINARY FORENSICS: 39-SCREEN BYTECODE STATE MACHINE              [ENGN]

A. THE LZSS 4096-BYTE RING BUFFER ARCHITECTURE:
  All compiled script chunks (.TOT) in INTRO.STK are packed with Coktel's
  proprietary LZSS variant:
  • Ring Buffer: 4096 bytes initialized with ASCII spaces (0x20).
  • Extended Mode: Magic 0x1234, 0x5678 expands max copy length from 18 to
    274 bytes.
  • Bitstream: Bit 1 = literal copy; Bit 0 = 12-bit offset + 4-bit length.

[10.00] CULTURAL RETROSPECTIVE: WHY WEEN REMAINED OBSCURE               [HIST]
===============================================================================

1. The French "Puzzle Chamber" Design: Coktel Vision prioritized dense,
   mathematically rigorous self-contained puzzle screens over open exploration.
2. Esoteric Reagents: From curing a sick worm with chamomile tea to glueing a
   firefly to a twig, puzzles required deep lateral thinking.
3. 1992 Sierra Lineup: Sierra focused their marketing budget on King's Quest VI
   and Space Quest V, leaving Ween as a hidden European cult classic.
