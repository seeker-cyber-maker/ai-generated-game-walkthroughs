[04.00] GAME BASICS, COMPANION ROLES & THE 30-DAY CLOCK                 [BASE]
===============================================================================

+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| THE THREE SACRED GRAINS OF SAND PROPHECY                                    |
|                                                                             |
| In the mystical Blue Land, the wicked sorcerer Kraal will unleash eternal   |
| darkness on the 30th day. Only Ween, guided by Petroy, can gather the       |
| 3 Grains of Sand and activate the Great Hourglass of Revuss in time!        |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------+

A. THE 30-DAY HARD TIME LIMIT:
  Every screen transition and rested night advances the internal clock. If Day
  30 arrives before placing all 3 Grains into the Great Hourglass, Kraal
  captures the kingdom, triggering a permanent game over.

B. COMPANION SPECIALIZATION MATRIX:
  • Ween (Protagonist): Picks up items, casts spells, grinds reagents.
  • Petroy (Mentor): Squeaks acoustic soundwaves, manipulates magical items.
  • Orain (Warrior): Lifts heavy stone blocks, operates forge bellows.
  • Urm (Trickster): Climbs trees, fits into tight crevices, steals items.

[05.01] ACT I: COTTAGE PORCH, FOREST & FIRST GRAIN                      [WLK01]
===============================================================================

+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| AREA ITEM CHECKLIST: ACT I                                                  |
|                                                                             |
| [ ] Straw Bundle ................. [Screen 01: Cottage Porch Floor]         |
| [ ] Wooden Sticks (x2) ........... [Screen 01: Porch Eaves & Woodpile]      |
| [ ] Wild Strawberries (x3) ....... [Screen 01: Flowerbed Path Patch]        |
| [ ] Brass Key .................... [Screen 02: Laboratory Fireplace]        |
| [ ] Mortar & Pestle .............. [Screen 02: Alchemist Worktable]         |
| [ ] Mandrake Root & Sulfur ....... [Screen 02: Cellar Storage Shelves]      |
| [ ] Woodpecker Feather ........... [Screen 03: Crossroads Hollow Oak]       |
| [ ] Sticky Amber Resin ........... [Screen 03: Pine Tree Trunk]             |
| [ ] [GRAIN 1] Golden Grain of Sand [Screen 04: Stone Golem Orgol's Chest]   |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------+

1. Screen 1 (Cottage Porch & Yard):
   - Gather the loose Straw Bundle from the broom on the porch floor.
   - Collect Wooden Sticks leaning against the woodpile beneath the eaves.
   - Pick the fresh Wild Strawberries growing along the garden pathway.
   - Use a Wooden Stick on the roof gutter to dislodge the stuck bronze coin.
   - Combine Stick + Straw + String in inventory to make a makeshift tool.
   - Feed a Strawberry to the creature near the doorstep to pacify it.
   - Unlatch the front door using the tool and step inside.

2. Screen 2 (Magician's Laboratory & Cellar):
   - Grab the Brass Key from the fireplace mantle.
   - Collect the Mortar & Pestle from the alchemist table.
   - Unlock the cellar trapdoor; take the Glass Flask, Dried Mandrake Root,
     and Sulfur Powder.

3. Screen 3 (Forest Crossroads & Hollow Oak):
   - Send Urm up the hollow oak tree to retrieve the Woodpecker Feather.
   - Slice the pine trunk with your knife to harvest sticky Amber Resin.
   - Grind Mandrake Root + Sulfur in Mortar to create Awakening Dust.

4. Screen 4 (Stone Golem Orgol's Shrine):
   - Blow Awakening Dust across the Stone Golem's eyes.
   - The Golem steps aside, unlocking the carved chest.
   - Open the chest to claim the First Sacred Grain of Sand (Golden Grain)!

| AREA ITEM CHECKLIST: ACT II                                                 |

| [ ] Molten Iron Crucible ......... [Screen 06: Magma Furnace Well]          |
| [ ] Ornate Dragon Key ............ [Screen 06: Quenched Cast Key]           |
| [ ] [GRAIN 2] Silver Grain of Sand [Screen 07: Dragon's Obsidian Altar]     |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------+

1. Screen 5 (Bat Cavern & Crystal Chasm):
   - Command Petroy to squeak; the acoustic echo scatters the bat swarm.
   - Scrape fresh Bat Guano from the cavern floor.
   - Set the Quartz Prism on the stone pedestal to reflect the sunbeam across
     the dark chasm bridge.

2. Screen 6 (Subterranean Smelting Forge):
   - Have Orain operate the forge lever to pump the bellows.
   - Pour Molten Iron into the Key Mold and quench in spring water to cast
     the Ornate Dragon Key.

3. Screen 7 (Dragon's Lair & Altar):
   - Throw sticky Amber Resin and a Wild Strawberry onto the rock ledge to
     distract the sleeping dragon.
   - Insert the Dragon Key into the obsidian pedestal to secure the Second
     Sacred Grain of Sand (Silver Grain)!

===============================================================================

+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| AREA ITEM CHECKLIST: ACT III                                                |

| [ ] Caustic Acid Flask ........... [Screen 08: Citadel Outer Ramparts]      |

| [ ] Mirror of Reflection ......... [Screen 09: Citadel Armory Rack]         |
| [ ] [GRAIN 3] Crystal Grain of Sand [Screen 09: Kraal's Vault]              |
| [ ] [VICTORY] The Prophecy Fulfilled [Screen 10: Great Hourglass of Revuss] |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------+

1. Screen 8 (Moat Drawbridge & Citadel Gate):
   - Pour Caustic Acid over the rusted drawbridge chains to melt the locks.
   - Send Urm through the arrow slit to attach the Counterweight Gear and
     lower the drawbridge.

2. Screen 9 (Kraal's Dark Laboratory):
   - Take the Mirror of Reflection from the armory rack.
   - Reflect Kraal's lethal shadow bolt back at him to shatter his shield.
   - Unlock Kraal's vault to take the Third Sacred Grain of Sand (Crystal)!

3. Screen 10 (Hourglass Tower & Finale):
   - Ascend to the pinnacle before Day 30 expires.
   - Solve the pendulum rope puzzle to release the temporal lock.
   - Insert all 3 Grains into the Hourglass of Revuss (Gold, Silver, Crystal).
   - Turn the Hourglass wheel to trap Kraal in a time loop and save the realm!

[08.00] ENGINE FORENSICS: COKTEL'S GOB ENGINE DECOMPILATION             [ENGN]
===============================================================================

A. GOB / STK ARCHITECTURE:
  • INTRO.STK (7.12 MB): Monolithic resource container containing all screen
    backgrounds, sprite animations, and audio effects.
  • ALL.ASK (11 KB): Bytecode script table evaluating triggers, companion
    actions, and state transitions.
  • *.GDR Display Drivers: LVGA.GDR (256-color VGA), LEGA.GDR (16-color EGA),
    and L360.GDR (Tweaked Mode-X).

B. SCUMMVM (GOB / WEEN) TARGET PROFILE:
  • Target Engine ID: gob (Sub-engine: ween).
  • CPU Timer Fix: Normalizes 30-day clock tick rate on modern systems.
  • Audio Emulation: Native support for AdLib OPL2 and Roland MT-32.
  • Savegame Format: Modern cross-platform save slots.

===============================================================================
[09.00] CULTURAL RETROSPECTIVE: WHY WEEN REMAINED OBSCURE               [HIST]
===============================================================================

1. The French "Puzzle Chamber" Design: Coktel Vision prioritized dense,
   mathematically rigorous self-contained puzzle screens over open exploration.
2. Severe Difficulty & Reagents: Multi-step chemical synthesis without modern
   hints challenged even veteran puzzle fans.
3. 1992 Sierra Lineup: Sierra focused their marketing budget on King's Quest VI
   and Space Quest V, leaving Ween as a hidden European cult classic.
