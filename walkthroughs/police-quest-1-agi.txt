[09.00] CHARACTER ORIGINS, SEQUELS & BLUE FORCE LINEAGE                 [LORE]
===============================================================================

A. REAL-LIFE INSPIRATIONS & SIERRA STUDIO IN-JOKES:
  • Art Serabian: Named after Arthur Serabian, real-life friend of Al Lowe.
    In the 1987 EGA release, Serabian's sprite was drawn as an exact self-
    caricature of Al Lowe himself (glasses, mustache, balding head).
  • Sonny Bonds: Named as a wordplay on "Bail Bonds" and Sonny Bono.
  • Helen Hots: Classic Al Lowe pun on "Hell on Wheels" / "Hell 'n Hot".
  • Sergeant Dooley: Modeled on real strict California Highway Patrol watch
    commanders Jim Walls served under during his 15 years on duty.
  • City of Lytton: Named after Lytton Springs in Sonoma County, California.
  • Caffeine Carol: Named after Carolly Hauks, Sierra Oakhurst office staff.

B. CHARACTER CONTINUITY IN SEQUELS & BLUE FORCE:
  1. Sonny Bonds:
     - PQ1 (1987): Patrolman -> Promoted to Narcotics Detective.
     - PQ2 (1988): Homicide Detective; tracks Bains and shoots him in finale.
     - PQ3 (1991): Promoted to Sergeant; marries Marie Wilkins.
  2. Marie Wilkins ("Sweet Cheeks"):
     - PQ1: High-school acquaintance turned hotel informant.
     - PQ2: Sonny's fiancée; kidnapped by Bains and rescued in catacombs.
     - PQ3: Sonny's wife; stabbed by Michael Bains' cult; in hospital coma.
  3. Officer Jack Cobb:
     - PQ1: Fellow officer at Blue Room whose daughter overdosed.
     - PQ2: Murdered by escaped convict Jessie Bains early in the game.
  4. Jessie Bains & Michael Bains:
     - PQ1: Primary villain ("The Death Angel"); arrested in Suite 216.
     - PQ2: Escapes prison with hit-list; slain by Sonny.
     - PQ3: Brother Michael Bains seeks vengeance through the Kindred cult.
  5. Jim Walls' Departure & Blue Force (1993, Tsunami Media):
     - Jim Walls left Sierra in 1992 and created Blue Force, starring rookie
       cop Jake Ryan and veteran mentor Lyle Jameson (successors to Bonds and
       Dooley/Cobb), retaining all authentic 10-codes and weapon safety checks.
