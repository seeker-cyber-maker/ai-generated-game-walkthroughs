[03.02] SHIELD PENETRATION & BEAM VOLLEYS                               [MAT02]
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

• LASER VOLLEYS: Every projectile strips exactly 1 shield bubble. To damage a
  4-shield Flagship, you must land at least 5 synchronized shots.
• BEAM FORMULA: Damage_Per_Room = Max(0, Beam_Base_Damage - Active_Shields).
• ION STACKING: 1 Ion damage removes 1 power bar for 5 seconds. Successive ion
  hits reset the dissipation timer and stack linearly!

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[03.03] FIRE SPREAD & O2 DEPLETION FSM                                  [MAT03]
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Fire spread probability is 5% per second to adjacent rooms when oxygen is above
10%. Depowering oxygen and venting rooms to space (0% O2) suffocates fires and
hostile boarders in under 4 seconds!

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[05.01] THE 3-STEP CRYSTAL CRUISER GEODESIC                             [CRY01]
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Crystal Cruiser (The Bravais, unlockShip id="8") is unlocked via a 3-part
quest chain spanning multiple sectors:

STEP 1: RECOVER DAMAGED STASIS POD
  • Location: Pirate, Engi, or Rock Controlled Sectors (Sectors 1-5).
  • Event: ASTEROID_SPACE_DOCK / PIRATE_SALVAGE.
  • Action: Blue Option req="rock" / req="ROCK_ARMOR" or search asteroid.
  • Reward: STASIS_POD augment ("Damaged Stasis Pod").

STEP 2: REVIVE RUWEN AT ZOLTAN/ENGI RESEARCH LAB
  • Location: Zoltan or Engi Sectors (Sectors 2-6).
  • Event: ZOLTAN_RESEARCH_LAB / ZOLTAN_SCIENCE_DOCK.
  • Action: Blue Option req="STASIS_POD" ("Ask if they can open the pod").
  • Reward: Revives Ruwen (Crystal crew member with Level 2 in 3 skills).

STEP 3: ACTIVATE ANCIENT DEVICE IN ROCK HOMEWORLDS
  • Location: Rock Homeworlds (Red Sector 4-7).
  • Mechanism: Ruwen forces a guaranteed green Quest Beacon on the map!
  • Event: ROCK_CRYSTAL_BEACON (req="crystal" -> "Reactivate ancient device").
  • Outcome: Teleports your ship into Sector 8: Hidden Crystal Worlds.

STEP 4: REACH CRYSTAL CITY BASE
  • Event: CRYSTAL_UNLOCK.
  • Reward: 10 Hull Repairs, CRYSTAL_SHARDS augment, and <unlockShip id="8"/>!

[05.02] ALL OTHER 8 CRUISER QUESTS & DIALOGUE TREES                     [CRY02]
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. STEALTH CRUISER (id="1", The Nesasio):
   Engi Homeworlds -> ENGI_UNLOCK_1 with Engi crew -> track 2 quest beacons to
   destroy secret Rebel Stealth Shipyard -> SYSTEM_CASING + unlockShip id="1".

2. MANTIS CRUISER (id="2", The Gila Monster):
   Mantis Homeworlds -> MANTIS_NAMED_THIEF (KazaaakplethKILIK) -> Kill crew
   WITHOUT destroying hull -> Medbay/Teleporter Lvl 2 -> Kazaaak joins crew!

3. FEDERATION CRUISER (id="4", The Osprey):
   Rebel Stronghold -> FLAGSHIP_CONSTRUCTION -> Destroy unfinished Flagship.

4. SLUG CRUISER (id="5", The Man of War):
   Slug Homeworlds -> JELLY_UNLOCK with Sensors Lvl 2 or Slug crew -> Reject
   weapon surrender ("We want data!") -> Defeat prototype in nebula.

5. ROCK CRUISER (id="6", The Bulwark):
   Rock Homeworlds -> ROCK_UNLOCK1 ("Save galaxy, not squabble") ->
   Endure solar flare without jumping early -> ROCK_ARMOR + unlockShip id="6".

6. ZOLTAN CRUISER (id="7", The Adjudicator):
   Zoltan Homeworlds -> ZOLTAN_PEACE_QUEST -> Choice 2 ("Hear them out") ->
   Choice 2 ("Resolve without violence") -> Choice 1 ("Progress without blood")
   -> Master Zoltan Envoy + ENERGY_SHIELD + unlockShip id="7".

7. ENGI CRUISER (The Torus): Reach Sector 5 with any ship.
8. LANIUS CRUISER (The Kruos): Unlock 4 non-Kestrel cruisers (in AE).

[08.00] THE REBEL FLAGSHIP: 3-PHASE DESTRUCTION GUIDE                   [BOSS]
===============================================================================

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[08.01] PHASE 1: CLOAKING & WEAPON ISOLATION                            [BOS01]
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Teleport into Room 3 (Triple Missile Artillery); eliminate operator.
2. Disable Ion Artillery (Room 1) and Beam Artillery (Room 4).
3. EXPLOIT: Leave the operator in Room 2 (Triple Laser) alive to prevent the
   Flagship automated AI from taking over!

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[08.02] PHASE 2: DEFENSE DRONE BYPASS & SURGE                           [BOS02]
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. HACKING DRONE TRICK: Depower Hacking as Defense Drone fires, then repower.
2. Time Level 1 Cloaking to activate when the Drone Power Surge alarm sounds.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[08.03] PHASE 3: ZOLTAN SHIELD & 12-LASER SURGE                         [BOS03]
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Strip 12 HP Zoltan Shield with rapid Flak and Laser fire.
2. Counter Mind Control using your own Mind Control or Slug crew.
3. Activate Level 1 Cloaking to dodge the 12-laser Power Surge volley!
