4. Engine Forensics & Maze Topology vs Indiana Jones [ENGN]

The Caverns of Twilight Illusion: A rigid 28-room directed graph reusing only 5 distinct 320x200 background art tiles, combined with a 3-step fireberry volatility countdown.

Comparison with LucasArts (SCUMM v5):

  • Kyrandia 1: Fixed room graph with seeded Birthstone Altar registers and a 16-room outdoor respawn pool.
  • Indiana Jones: Fate of Atlantis: Dynamically seeded Plato stone disk alignments and a 4-room candidate pool for key items across 3 branching paths (Wits, Fists, Team).
