2. Complete Step-by-Step Walkthrough [WLK00]

Act I: Timbermist Woods & Brandon's Awakening [WLK01]

Area Item Checklist: Act I
  1. Take the Note from Kallak and Apple in the cabin. Inspect the outer tree bark to find the Hand Saw.
  2. Visit the Temple of the Sun. Place a healing flower into the altar basin to unlock the Yellow Healing Spell.
  3. Return Merith's lost item to receive the Teardrop Stone.

Act II: The Great Tree & Serpent's Grotto [WLK02]

Area Item Checklist: Act II
  1. Cast Yellow Healing on the poisoned well. The serpent retreats, revealing the Sunstone (Fixed Gem #1).
  2. Use the Saw on the dead branch to retrieve the Ruby (Fixed Gem #4).
  3. Place Sunstone (1), Gem A (2), Gem B (3), and Ruby (4) on the Marble Altar to awaken the Purple Wisp Transformation Spell.

Act III: Caverns of Twilight & Firefly Labyrinth [WLK03]

Area Item Checklist: Act III
  1. Carry firefly berries in your hand and place them in nests to illuminate dark rooms.
  2. Pay the ferryman with a gold Coin or cast Wisp. Use the Iron Key to unlock the chest and retrieve the Magic Flute.

Act IV & V: Zanthia's Realm & Malcolm's Castle [WLK04/WLK05]

Area Item Checklist: Acts IV & V
  1. Brew the Blue Flying Potion (Water + Blue Gem) and drink it to cross the river canyon.
  2. Unlock the Red Invisibility Spell on your amulet. Collect the 3 Royal Regalia: Scepter, Crown, and Chalice.
  3. Silence the gate gargoyles with the Magic Flute. Place the 3 Regalia on the throne cushions.
  4. The Malcolm Finale:
    • Cast Red Invisibility when Malcolm attacks.
    • Walk over to the large Magic Mirror on the right wall.
    • Malcolm casts petrification; the mirror reflects the beam back, turning Malcolm into solid stone!

5. Engine Forensics: The Gem Drop & Altar RNG [ENGN]

How the 4-Slot Birthstone Altar evaluates gemstones in KYRA.EXE:

Slot 1: 0x01 -> SUNSTONE (100% Deterministic & Hardcoded)
Slot 2: RND  -> Random Gem A (Chosen at new game start via Seed % 10)
Slot 3: RND  -> Random Gem B (Chosen at new game start via (Seed + 3) % 10)
Slot 4: 0x02 -> RUBY (100% Deterministic & Hardcoded)

Forest Respawn Pool:
When an incorrect gem is rejected, it teleports to:
Target_Room = Forest_Respawn_Vector[NextSeed % 16]
