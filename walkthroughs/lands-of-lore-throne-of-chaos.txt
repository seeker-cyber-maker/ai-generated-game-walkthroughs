[03.00] GAME BASICS & CHARACTER SELECTION                               [CHAR]
===============================================================================

At character creation, you select one of four champions:

+-----------+--------------+--------------+--------------+--------------+
| Attribute |   Ak'shel    |   Michael    |    Kieran    |    Conrad    |
+-----------+--------------+--------------+--------------+--------------+
| Race/Role | Dracoid Mage | Human Knight | Thomgog Rogue| Human All-Rd |
| Base HP   | 45           | 85           | 60           | 65           |
| Base Mana | 75           | 15           | 35           | 45           |
| Might     | 8 (Low)      | 18 (Max)     | 12 (Medium)  | 14 (High)    |
| Agility   | 10 (Medium)  | 10 (Medium)  | 18 (Max)     | 14 (High)    |
| Magic     | 18 (Max)     | 4 (Low)      | 8 (Medium)   | 12 (High)    |
| Passives  | 2x Passive   | +20% Melee   | Fast Attack  | Balanced     |
| Trait     | Mana Regen   | Stun/Knockbk | Speed + Lock | Skill Growth |
+-----------+--------------+--------------+--------------+--------------+

CRITICAL GAME MECHANICS:
1. Lockpicking vs Bashing: Michael can force-bash doors, but force-bashing
   locked treasure chests shatters fragile glass potion vials inside into
   useless residue. Kieran's lockpicking guarantees 100% loot recovery.
2. Baccata's 4-Arm Multiplier: Baccata possesses 4 independent arm slots. He
   can wield two 2-handed weapons or 4 single-handed blades/wands at once.
3. Spell Charging: Spells are charged from Level 1 to 4 by holding down the
   mouse button over the spell glyph before releasing.

[04.01] ACT I: GLADSTONE KEEP & THE GREAT FORESTS                       [WLK01]
===============================================================================

+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| AREA ITEM & CHEST CHECKLIST: GLADSTONE & FORESTS                            |
|                                                                             |
| [ ] Gladstone Signet Ring ... (X:02, Y:01) [NPC: King Richard]             |
| [ ] Timothy (Companion) ..... (X:15, Y:01) [NPC: Hallway Soldier]          |
| [ ] [SECRET] Fine Broadsword  (X:08, Y:02) [Hidden Alcove: Torch Switch]    |
| [ ] [SECRET] Leather Cuirass. (X:08, Y:02) [Hidden Alcove: Torch Switch]    |
| [ ] Aloe Leaf (x3) .......... (X:08, Y:02) [Hidden Alcove: Torch Switch]    |
| [ ] [MISSABLE] Elven Longbow. (X:14, Y:08) [Northlands: 3-Click Oak Knot]  |
| [ ] Lightning Quiver (x20) .. (X:14, Y:08) [Northlands: 3-Click Oak Knot]  |
| [ ] [PERMANENT BUFF] Blue Well(X:04, Y:12) [Roland Manor: +5 Max Mana]      |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------+

1. Gladstone Briefing: Speak to King Richard in the throne room for the
   commission and Signet Ring. Recruit Timothy in the east corridor.
2. Secret Cellar Stash: In the dungeon cellar hallway, click the third wall
   torch at (X:08, Y:02) to open the secret alcove (Fine Broadsword & Leather
   Cuirass).
3. Northlands Forest Secret: Travel to (X:14, Y:08). Click the hollow knot on
   the third ancient oak tree EXACTLY 3 TIMES to open the clearing containing
   the Elven Longbow of Storms and Lightning Arrows.

   *** POINT OF NO RETURN ***
   You MUST collect the Elven Longbow before completing Roland's Manor. Once
   Scotia attacks, the forest burns and the bow is permanently missable!

4. Roland's Manor Ambush: Drink from the Blue Fountain at (X:04, Y:12) (+5 Max
   Mana permanent). Scotia strikes, stealing the Nether Mask and poisoning
   King Richard. Timothy departs/falls.

| [ ] [STORY CURE] Bloodmoss .. (X:22, Y:19) [Swamp Node]                     |

1. Recruit Baccata: Meet Baccata at the Southlands outpost. Equip him with

2. Grey Eagle Inn: Recruit Lora the Healer. Buy the Compass from the merchant.
3. Gorkha Swamp Core: Drink from the Green Fountain at (X:08, Y:19) (+5 Max HP
   permanent).
4. Shaman's Gate & Bloodmoss: Deliver a Ruby to the Shaman at (X:18, Y:04) for
   the healing herb and gate access. Harvest Bloodmoss at (X:22, Y:19).

3. Draracle Trials: Complete the Scale Tribute, answer the Sphinx riddle, and
   walk through the illusion wall at (X:24, Y:10). Lora is abducted by Scotia.

3. Synthesize King's Elixir: Combine Crucible of Faith, Sweet Water,
   Bloodmoss, and Silver Goblet in inventory to brew the Elixir of Arin. Cure
   King Richard to receive the Ruby of Truth.

[04.06] ACT VI: CASTLE CIMMERIA & THE NETHER MASK FINALE                [WLK06]
===============================================================================

+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| AREA ITEM & CHEST CHECKLIST: CASTLE CIMMERIA                                |
|                                                                             |
| [ ] Greatsword of Slaying ... (X:04, Y:09) [Cimmeria L1: Weapon Rack]       |
| [ ] Dragon Scale Armor ...... (X:18, Y:12) [Cimmeria L2: Golem Chamber]     |

   - Phase 1: Click Ruby of Truth to strip her Nether Mask and beast forms.
   - Phase 2: Activate Vaelan's Cube to reflect her Death Magic back onto her
     while attacking with all party arms to finish the game.

[05.00] THE CRITICAL-PATH MINIMALIST ROUTE                              [FAST]
===============================================================================

For speedrunners and fast replays. Skip all optional chests, side quests, and
dialogue trees. Execute strictly the following state transitions:

STEP 01: [Gladstone Keep] ────► Walk to (X:02, Y:01), talk to Richard (Flag).
STEP 02: [Roland Manor] ──────► Travel to (X:04, Y:12), trigger Scotia scene.
STEP 03: [Southlands] ────────► Walk to (X:03, Y:11), recruit Baccata.
STEP 04: [Gorkha Swamp] ──────► Walk to (X:18, Y:04), give Ruby to Shaman.
STEP 05: [Gorkha Mire] ───────► Walk to (X:22, Y:19), harvest Bloodmoss.
STEP 06: [Urbish Mines L3] ───► Run to (X:16, Y:28), pick up Crucible of Faith.
STEP 07: [Draracle Lair] ─────► Drop 15 gold coins on scale at (X:09, Y:14).
STEP 08: [Draracle Lair] ─────► Choose "Truth" at riddle gate (X:16, Y:10).
STEP 09: [Draracle Lair] ─────► Walk through illusion wall at (X:24, Y:10).
STEP 10: [White Tower L2] ────► Use empty Flask at cistern (X:08, Y:14).
STEP 11: [White Tower L3] ────► Recruit Paul or Dawn at (X:16, Y:04).
STEP 12: [Gladstone Treasury] ► Grab Silver Goblet at (X:02, Y:03).
STEP 13: [Inventory Screen] ──► Combine Crucible + Water + Moss + Goblet.
STEP 14: [Gladstone Throne] ──► Feed Elixir to King Richard -> Get Ruby.
STEP 15: [Yvel Elders] ───────► Enter room at (X:15, Y:02) -> Get Cube & Key.
STEP 16: [Castle Cimmeria L1] ► Dispel barrier at (X:16, Y:08) with Cube.
STEP 17: [Castle Cimmeria L3] ► Enter Scotia's Arena at (X:16, Y:16).
STEP 18: [Boss Arena] ────────► Use Ruby -> Use Cube -> Attack crone -> WIN.

[08.00] ENGINE FORENSICS & REVERSE-ENGINEERING                          [ENGN]
===============================================================================

16-Bit Tile Bitmask in LEVELxx.INF:
  Bit 6 (0x0040): Illusion Wall (Passable = TRUE)
  Bit 7 (0x0080): Switch Trigger (Trips TRIG.TBL event)

Decompiled Monster Drop RNG (Borland C++ LCG in LANDS.EXE):
  NextSeed = (Seed * 1103515245 + 12345) % (2^31)
  Roll = (NextSeed >> 16) % 100
