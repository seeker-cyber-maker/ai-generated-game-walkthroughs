===============================================================================
[04.01] ACT I: GLADSTONE KEEP & THE GREAT FORESTS                       [WLK01]
===============================================================================

1. Gladstone Keep: Speak with King Richard (voiced by Patrick Stewart) in the
   Throne Room. Accept the Royal Commission Letter and 50 Gold Sovereigns.
2. Armory: Equip the Iron Broadsword, Leather Jerkin, and Wooden Shield.
3. North Forest: Navigate through the woods, gathering Ginseng and Aloe. Defeat
   roaming Thugs and Goblins using hit-and-run strafing tactics.
4. Southlands: Meet Baccata the Umpani warrior at the campfire. Recruit him
   into your party to absorb front-line melee damage.

===============================================================================
[04.02] ACT II: GORKHA SWAMP & SHAMAN'S TRIAL                           [WLK02]
===============================================================================

1. Enter Gorkha Swamp. Cast Spark to dispatch poisonous Leech swarms.
2. Visit the Gorkha Village; speak to the High Shaman.
3. Complete the Trial of Courage in sunken ruins to claim the Ruby of Truth.

===============================================================================
[04.03] ACT III: MINES OF APPARITIONS & DRARACLE'S LAIR                 [WLK03]
===============================================================================

1. Descend into the Mines of Apparitions. Equip Silver weapons against Ghosts.
2. Solve the Pit Trap alignment puzzle by throwing rocks onto pressure plates.
3. Enter the Draracle's Lair. Consult the ancient seer to discover Scotia's
   weakness: the legendary Ring of the Nexus!

===============================================================================
[04.04] ACT IV: THE WHITE TOWER & SIEGE OF GLADSTONE                    [WLK04]
===============================================================================

1. Ascend the three tiers of the White Tower. Solve the stained-glass puzzle.
2. Defeat the Minotaur Guard to secure the Elixir of Life.
3. Return to Gladstone Keep under siege; administer the Elixir to save King
   Richard from Scotia's deadly poison!

===============================================================================
[04.05] ACT V: CITY OF YVEL & THE SEWERS                                [WLK05]
===============================================================================

1. Travel to the besieged City of Yvel. Defeat Scotia's dark vanguard.
2. Enter the Yvel Sewers; navigate the submerged tunnels using the Water Key.
3. Locate the hidden resistance armory and retrieve the Dragon Gauntlet.

===============================================================================
[04.06] ACT VI: CASTLE CIMMERIA & NETHER MASK FINALE                    [WLK06]
===============================================================================

1. Infiltrate Castle Cimmeria. Solve the dimensional teleport labyrinth.
2. Confront Scotia in the Throne of Chaos.
3. When Scotia equips the Nether Mask to shapeshift, use the Ruby of Truth to
   strip her illusions, then strike her down with the Ancient Broadsword!

[08.00] THE MANUALS, LORE BOOKS, FEELIES & COPY PROTECTION              [MANL]
===============================================================================

A. RETAIL COPY-PROTECTION SYSTEM:
  In original floppy releases, the game prompted for specific words from the
  48-page manual (e.g., "Page 14, Line 3, Word 4").

B. X86 DISASSEMBLY & PATCH VECTORS:
  In x86 DOS binaries, manual checks evaluated a conditional jump:
    CALL CheckWordMatch        ; Returns AX=1 (Valid), AX=0 (Invalid)
    TEST AX, AX
    JNZ  PassProtection        ; Machine Code: 75 0C (Jump if Non-Zero)
  Crackers historically bypassed this via 3 methods:
    1. Forced Jump: Patching 75 0C (JNZ) to EB 0C (JMP Short).
    2. NOP Fallthrough: Patching 74 08 (JZ Fail) to 90 90 (NOP NOP).
    3. Function Stubbing: Overwriting entry with B0 01 C3 (MOV AL, 1; RET).

[10.00] SEQUEL BRIDGES, CHARACTER EVOLUTION & WESTWOOD LORE             [SEQL]
===============================================================================

A. SCOTIA'S LEGACY: LUTHER & THE SHAPESHIFTING CURSE (LANDS OF LORE II):
  Defeating Scotia in Castle Cimmeria does not eradicate the power of the
  Nether Mask. In "Lands of Lore II: Guardians of Destiny" (1997), Scotia's
  son, Luther, inherits an uncontrollable genetic curse that forces him to
  spontaneously shapeshift between human, a hulking beast, and a lizard.

B. FALL OF GLADSTONE: COPPER LEGRE & THE RIFT BEASTS (LANDS OF LORE III):
  In "Lands of Lore III" (1999), Gladstone Keep is shattered when dimensional
  rifts tear open across the realm. King Richard is slain, and the player takes
  control of Copper LeGre (illegitimate son of Eric LeGre and nephew to
  King Richard) to heal the fractured universe.

C. THE DRARACLE & ARRANT RRAK / ARRANT PRAC ANAGRAMS:
  Across all three games, the Draracle (Dragon + Oracle) acts as an immortal
  cosmic architect. Westwood developers encoded linguistic in-jokes into his
  ancient lizard disciple: "Arrant Rrak" (and its anagrammatic merchant variant
  "Arrant Prac"), who recurs as a cryptic shopkeeper and scholar.
